# Audio sources

- `pickup.wav`, `drop.wav`, and `hint.wav` are converted from Kenney's `UI Audio` pack (`mouseclick1.ogg`, `mouserelease1.ogg`, and `rollover1.ogg`). The pack is CC0. [Asset page](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio) · [License](https://kenney.nl/support)
- `snap-click.wav` is from qubodup's `Click` sound on OpenGameArt, released as CC0. [Asset page](https://opengameart.org/content/click)
- `snap.wav` and `complete.wav` are original procedural bell accents generated by `Tools/generate_audio.py`.

The Kenney and OpenGameArt sources do not require attribution. Credits are recorded here for provenance.
